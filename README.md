# CoinGlass how about it? CoinGlass introduction and review

## CoinGlass Introduction

CoinGlass is a professional cryptocurrency derivatives data analysis platform that serves as a comprehensive resource for traders seeking accurate, real-time cryptocurrency market data and analysis services. The platform specializes in providing extensive data analytics for the cryptocurrency derivatives market, offering crucial insights into pivotal market metrics such as open interest, funding rates, liquidation volumes, and long-short ratios across various cryptocurrencies. CoinGlass integrates multi-dimensional market information including price trends, crypto contract trading volume, open interest, funding rates, liquidation data, and options data, creating a comprehensive and intuitive market perspective for users. The platform supports tracking of over 7,000 cryptocurrencies including Bitcoin, Ethereum, Binance Coin, Ripple, Cardano, Dogecoin, and many others, while monitoring over 38,000 active cryptocurrency markets.
